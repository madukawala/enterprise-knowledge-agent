# Instant Payments Gateway and FastLane Integration

## Purpose and scope

This document describes the Instant Payments Gateway (IPG), which connects PayHub to FastLane, the national instant payment scheme operated by the clearing house NorthClear. It covers outbound payments sent by Helix customers and inbound payments received from other banks. It does not cover the standard clearing route used for bulk and scheduled payments.

## Context

Helix Instant Transfer (PS-001) lets customers send up to the scheme limit to any participating bank, with funds arriving within seconds at any time of day. The scheme rules require the sending bank to receive a final answer within 10 seconds. If no answer arrives in time, the payment is treated as rejected and must not be resent automatically under the same scheme reference.

We send about 420,000 instant payments on a normal day and receive about 380,000. Volume grows by roughly 25% a year.

## Components

| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| `ipg-connector` | Holds the connections to the FastLane gateway, signs and sends messages, receives responses. Kubernetes, namespace `ipg`. |
| `ipg-inbound` | Receives incoming credit transfers, checks the account with CoreLedger and replies to the scheme. |
| `ipg-store` | Postgres database recording every message sent and received with the scheme reference. |
| Scheme certificates | Signing and transport certificates issued by NorthClear, stored in the HSM. |
| Liquidity monitor | Tracks our settlement position at NorthClear and alerts Treasury when it falls below the threshold. |

## Data flow

Outbound payment:

1. PayHub routes an instant payment to IPG after funds are reserved in CoreLedger.
2. `ipg-connector` builds the scheme message, signs it and sends it to the FastLane gateway.
3. NorthClear passes the message to the receiving bank and waits for its answer.
4. NorthClear returns accepted or rejected, with a reason code, to IPG.
5. IPG records the result in `ipg-store` and returns it to PayHub, which completes or fails the payment.
6. If no answer arrives within 10 seconds, IPG marks the payment as timed out and PayHub fails it. The customer sees an error and is asked to try again.

Inbound payment:

1. NorthClear sends a credit transfer to `ipg-inbound`.
2. IPG checks the account exists and is open through the CoreLedger API.
3. IPG replies accepted and CoreLedger credits the account. The customer is notified through the Payment Status Service.

## Resilience and failure modes

| Failure | Effect today |
|---|---|
| NorthClear slow | Payments time out at 10 seconds and fail. Customers retry. |
| NorthClear unavailable | All outbound instant payments fail until the scheme returns. |
| Receiving bank down | Payments to that bank are rejected by the scheme with a reason code. |
| One IPG pod lost | Other pods continue; in-flight messages are recovered from `ipg-store`. |
| CoreLedger slow on inbound | Inbound payments may be rejected back to the sender. |

IPG runs four pods across two availability zones at the primary site, with a cold standby at the secondary site.

## Security

- Messages are signed with a key held in the HSM. NorthClear rejects unsigned or wrongly signed messages.
- The connection to NorthClear is over a private network link with mTLS.
- Routing rules that send payments to IPG are held in the PayHub configuration service and changes are subject to POL-005.

## Known limitations

- IPG has no degraded mode. When NorthClear is slow, payments fail rather than wait, and customer retries add to the load.
- Timeouts are not separated from rejections on the main dashboard, which makes diagnosis slower.
- The secondary site standby is cold and takes about 45 minutes to bring up.
- There is no agreed service level for scheme response time in the current NorthClear contract. Kwame Asante is leading the contract review.
