## Discussion

### Payment incidents in Q4 2025

There was one payment-failure incident in the quarter: INC-2025-041 on 3 December 2025, SEV1. Card payments were declined for a large share of customers because the primary mTLS certificate between CardSwitch and the Network Gateway expired. Liam reminded the group that this was the first SEV1 on the card side since the HSM firmware problem in July (INC-2025-022), which had a different cause and is now closed.

Hannah noted that one incident in a quarter sounds good, but this one was entirely avoidable and was visible to customers at the busiest time of day. She asked that the review look at causes, not just counts.

### Certificate expiry at the Network Gateway

Elena walked through what happened. The certificate had a known expiry date, but nobody was alerted. The renewal reminder sat in a shared mailbox that the team stopped reading after a reorganisation. There is no automated check that compares certificate expiry dates against a threshold.

Points raised:

- The renewal procedure lives in people's heads. Elena's team is writing it up as RB-002 and expects to publish it by mid-January.
- Triage was slow because the first alerts looked like a network problem. Tomasz proposed a card authorisation triage runbook. RB-007 was published in December and the group agreed it should be the starting point for any card decline spike.
- Automated certificate expiry monitoring was promised in the INC-2025-041 follow-ups. Tomasz said a Prometheus exporter for certificate expiry is on the Site Reliability backlog but not yet started. Hannah asked for a firm date.
- Mei Lin asked whether the secondary CardSwitch site uses the same certificates. Elena said it has its own copy and the renewal was done on both sites by hand. This should be covered in RB-002.

### Follow-up from the quarter-end PayHub slowdown

INC-2025-031 (29 September 2025, SEV3) fell in Q3 but its actions were due in Q4. PayHub slowed down at quarter-end because the `payhub-db` connection pool came close to its limit of 200 connections per pod group. At the time it was treated as a one-off.

Priya said the actions were only partly done. A slow query on the `payment_instructions` table was identified but not fixed. No load test was run before the December month-end. December was fine, but traffic was lower because of the holidays. Daniel was concerned that salary day on 30 January will be the first real test.

### Readiness for salary day and month-end

- Daniel confirmed the PeopleHub payroll file for January is expected on 29 January, which is normal.
- Tomasz will add a Grafana panel showing pool usage per pod group, with an alert at 80 per cent.
- Priya wants the `payment_instructions` index in place before salary day. Her team thinks it is a small change but it needs a change slot.

### Format of this review

The group agreed to hold this review every quarter, about two weeks after quarter end. Each review will list the quarter's payment incidents, group them by cause, and track open follow-ups from earlier quarters. Liam will keep a running tracker.

## Decisions

1. The Payments Reliability Review becomes a standing quarterly meeting chaired by the Head of Payments Platform.
2. Automated certificate expiry monitoring is the top reliability action for Cards Platform and Site Reliability in Q1 2026.
3. RB-007 is the agreed first step for any spike in card declines.
4. The `payment_instructions` index must be deployed before 30 January 2026.
