## Discussion

### Q1 2026 payment incidents by cause

Four payment-failure incidents in the quarter, compared with one in Q4 2025. One SEV1 and three SEV2.

| Incident | Date | Severity | Cause |
|---|---|---|---|
| INC-2026-003 | 30 Jan | SEV1 | PayHub database connection pool exhausted on salary day |
| INC-2026-007 | 17 Feb | SEV2 | IPG routing rule changed in production without a canary |
| INC-2026-009 | 27 Feb | SEV2 | PayHub connection pool exhausted at month-end, bulk payments delayed |
| INC-2026-012 | 16 Mar | SEV2 | FastLane slowed down at NorthClear, instant payments timed out |

Two of the four had the same cause as the quarter-end slowdown in September 2025.

### Connection pool exhaustion at peak

The `payment_instructions` index went in on 6 February after the review of INC-2026-003 (MTG-002). Query times dropped sharply. Even so, INC-2026-009 happened at month-end three weeks later.

Priya explained why. The load test before month-end was run, but it used the retail transfer profile from salary day. Month-end is different: large bulk payment files from business customers arrive in a short window and each file holds connections for longer. The pool of 200 per pod group filled up again, though for a shorter time than in January.

- RB-001 was published on 5 March. Payments Operations used it during a near miss on 31 March and it worked: bulk file intake was throttled and pods were not restarted.
- Sofia's capacity review says the database can safely support a larger pool, but recommends moving read-only queries off the primary in the longer term.

### Configuration change without a canary

INC-2026-007 was covered in detail at the Change Advisory Board on 19 March (MTG-003). Alejandro confirmed the POL-005 revision was approved this week. Configuration changes affecting payments and cards now need a canary and a written rollback plan. RB-005 is published. Admin screens in Sentinel and Scheduler still lack partial rollout and rely on the interim two-person check.

### FastLane timeouts and the new degraded mode

During INC-2026-012, NorthClear's FastLane service slowed down and many requests hit the 10-second scheme timeout. IPG had no degraded mode, so payments failed outright and customers had to try again later.

RB-003 was published on 8 April. It introduces a queue-and-retry degraded mode: when timeouts pass a threshold, IPG holds new instant payments in a queue, tells the customer the payment is being processed, and retries once the scheme recovers. Payments that cannot be sent within the retry window are returned to the customer with a clear message.

Kwame said NorthClear's incident report blamed a capacity problem on their side. He has asked for their capacity plan and a commitment on timeout rates, and will set up a formal vendor review if there is another event.

### Early Q2 incident on the secondary CardSwitch site

INC-2026-015 happened on 9 April and will be counted in Q2, but Elena gave an early view. Card payments failed for traffic routed to the secondary CardSwitch site because the intermediate certificate was missing from its trust chain after a renewal. This is the second certificate incident since INC-2025-041.

The expiry monitoring promised after INC-2025-041 went live in March, but it only checks the leaf certificate on the primary site. It does not check the full chain or the secondary site. Hannah said this is the second time the action has been reported as done when it was only partly done.

## Decisions

1. Month-end load tests must include a bulk-file profile.
2. The pool size will be reviewed again after the April and May month-ends.
3. IPG degraded mode from RB-003 is to be switched on automatically, not by hand, by the end of May.
4. Certificate monitoring is not closed until it checks the full chain on both CardSwitch sites.
