## Discussion

### Q2 2026 payment incidents by cause

Four payment-failure incidents, the same number as Q1, with one SEV1. Three of the four repeat causes seen before. The Kafka lag problem is new.

| Incident | Date | Severity | Cause |
|---|---|---|---|
| INC-2026-015 | 9 Apr | SEV2 | Intermediate certificate missing on the secondary CardSwitch site |
| INC-2026-017 | 22 Apr | SEV2 | Kafka consumer lag in the Payment Status Service; statuses stuck at pending, duplicates |
| INC-2026-021 | 29 May | SEV2 | PayHub connection pool exhausted at month-end |
| INC-2026-024 | 11 Jun | SEV1 | FastLane scheme outage at NorthClear |

Over the last three quarters the tracker shows three connection pool incidents, two certificate incidents, two FastLane incidents, one configuration change incident and one Kafka lag incident.

### Certificates on the secondary CardSwitch site

INC-2026-015 was the second certificate incident after INC-2025-041. RB-002 has been updated to check the full chain on both sites after any renewal.

Tomasz reported that certificate monitoring now checks the full chain, not just the leaf, on the primary site. The secondary site is still not covered, because the exporter cannot reach it through the current firewall rules in the segmented card network. A firewall change is with Fraud and Security for approval. This action was first raised in December. Elena agreed to do a manual check of the secondary site every week until monitoring is live.

### Payment status lag and duplicate payments

In INC-2026-017, the Payment Status Service fell behind on the `payments.status` Kafka topic after a spike in volume. Payments completed, but customers saw "pending" in Helix Mobile for up to an hour. Many retried and created duplicate payments, which Payments Operations then had to recall.

- RB-004 was published on 14 May and covers how to spot and clear consumer lag.
- Autoscaling of PSS consumers based on lag is in testing and due live by end of July.
- Idempotency keys on payment submission, so that a retry with the same key cannot create a second payment, are designed but need changes in Helix Mobile, Helix Online and PayHub. Target is Q3.

### Month-end connection pool, again

INC-2026-021 was shorter than the January and February incidents. The bulk-file load test had passed, but a new large business customer onboarded in May sent a much bigger file than expected.

After the incident, the pool was raised from 200 to 350 connections per pod group, in line with Sofia's capacity review. Priya warned that raising the pool buys time but does not fix the design: reporting and status queries still run on the primary database. She proposed moving read-only queries to a read replica.

### FastLane outage follow-up

Kwame summarised the NorthClear vendor review (MTG-006). The root-cause report has arrived and is being assessed. The automatic degraded mode from RB-003 worked as designed during INC-2026-024. Long-outage handling is being designed.

### Early Q3: fraud threshold change

Arjun gave an early view of INC-2026-027 on 8 July, which will be counted in Q3. A card-not-present fraud threshold in Sentinel was changed through the admin screen and applied to all traffic at once, declining many genuine online card payments. The revised POL-005 requires a canary, but Sentinel's admin screen still cannot do a partial rollout, and the interim two-person check was skipped because the change was treated as routine tuning. This is the same pattern as INC-2026-007.

## Decisions

1. Approve the read-replica design for PayHub read-only queries, target before the August month-end.
2. Weekly manual certificate check on the secondary site until automated monitoring covers it.
3. Idempotency keys are a Q3 priority for Payments Platform and the channel teams.
4. No Sentinel threshold change without the two-person check until partial rollout exists.
