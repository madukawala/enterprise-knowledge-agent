## Discussion

### Q3 2026 payment incidents by cause

Four payment-failure incidents, the same as the previous two quarters. One SEV1, two SEV2 and one SEV3.

| Incident | Date | Severity | Cause |
|---|---|---|---|
| INC-2026-027 | 8 Jul | SEV2 | Sentinel fraud threshold changed for all traffic without a canary |
| INC-2026-030 | 14 Aug | SEV3 | Kafka consumer lag in the Payment Status Service, delayed notifications |
| INC-2026-032 | 31 Aug | SEV2 | PayHub connection pool exhausted at month-end |
| INC-2026-035 | 18 Sep | SEV1 | Renewed Network Gateway certificate deployed to the primary site only |

### Third certificate incident and monitoring

INC-2026-035 is the third certificate incident since INC-2025-041 in December, after INC-2026-015 in April. The planned renewal in September was carried out on the primary CardSwitch site, but the deployment step for the secondary site failed silently. When traffic moved to the secondary site during routine maintenance, card payments were declined against the old certificate.

The weekly manual check was missed that week, and the secondary site exporter, approved in late August, was not yet switched on.

Since the incident:

- Automated certificate monitoring now covers the full chain on both sites, with alerts at 30, 14 and 7 days, and a check that both sites present the same certificate serial number.
- RB-002 now ends with a mandatory test transaction through each site.

The group agreed the action first raised after INC-2025-041 is now complete.

### Connection pool and the read replica

INC-2026-032 happened at the August month-end, before the read replica was live. The pool at 350 per pod group held for longer than before but still ran out when reporting queries and bulk files overlapped. After the incident the read replica change was brought forward and went live on 10 September. Balance checks, status lookups and reporting queries now read from the replica.

The September month-end was clean. Peak pool usage was 58 per cent, the lowest at any month-end this year. Priya proposed closing the connection pool theme after one more clean month-end.

### Fraud threshold change without a canary

INC-2026-027 followed the same pattern as INC-2026-007: a configuration change went to all production traffic at once. Arjun reported that partial rollout for Sentinel thresholds went live on 15 September. Changes now apply to 5 per cent of traffic for at least 30 minutes before full release, in line with POL-005.

Alejandro noted that the Scheduler admin screen is the last payment system without a canary or partial rollout. It is scheduled for Q4.

### Payment status lag and idempotency keys

PSS consumer autoscaling went live in late July. In INC-2026-030, lag still built up because the consumer count reached the number of partitions on `payments.status` and could not scale further. Only notifications were delayed. Partitions have since been increased.

Idempotency keys are live in Helix Mobile and PayHub. Helix Online will follow in November. Daniel confirmed duplicate payment recalls have dropped sharply since Mobile went live.

### Early Q4: FastLane timeouts

INC-2026-038 on 6 October will be counted in Q4. FastLane had intermittent timeouts for about 90 minutes. IPG entered degraded mode automatically, payments were queued and sent on retry, and almost no customer payments failed. NorthClear notified participants within its new 15-minute commitment. This is the first FastLane event handled without customer harm.

### Year-to-date view of repeated causes

Liam presented the tracker from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026: four connection pool incidents, three certificate incidents, two FastLane incidents, two configuration change incidents and two Kafka lag incidents. Most fixes are in place. Still open: the Scheduler canary and Helix Online idempotency keys.

## Decisions

1. Close the certificate monitoring action raised after INC-2025-041.
2. Close the connection pool theme if the October month-end is clean.
3. Scheduler partial rollout is the top configuration item for Q4.
4. Present the year-end summary at the Q4 review in January.
