# HR Town Hall Questions and Answers

## Meeting details

- Date: 10 September 2026, 12:30 to 13:30
- Location: Northbridge head office atrium, with a live stream for all staff
- Host: Grace Mensah, HR Director
- About 1,100 staff joined in person or online. Questions were submitted in advance and from the floor.

## Attendees

- Grace Mensah, HR Director (host)
- Hannah Schulz, Head of Operational Risk and Compliance
- Priya Raman, Head of Payments Platform (representing the technology leadership team)
- Members of the HR Systems team (handling online questions)

## Agenda

1. Opening remarks
2. Hybrid working
3. Pay and benefits
4. Learning and development
5. Wellbeing
6. Technology and acceptable use
7. Closing remarks

## Discussion

### Opening remarks

Grace thanked staff for a busy year. She said the staff survey response rate was the highest yet at 78 per cent, and that the top three themes were flexibility, career development and workload. Today's session was built around those themes.

### Hybrid working

**Q: Will the expectation of two office days a week change?**
Grace said no change is planned for 2026. The Remote Working Policy (POL-009) still sets a minimum of two days a week in the office for most roles, agreed within each team. Teams may choose which days. Some roles, such as branch staff, have different arrangements.

**Q: Can I work from another country for a few weeks?**
Short periods of up to 20 working days a year may be approved by a line manager, provided tax and data rules allow it. Requests must be made at least four weeks in advance through the HR portal.

**Q: The office is too full on Tuesdays and Wednesdays.**
Grace accepted this. The facilities team will add bookable desks on the third floor from October and will share weekly occupancy figures so teams can spread their office days.

### Pay and benefits

**Q: When will the annual pay review happen?**
Pay review letters will be sent in March 2027, with new salaries from 1 April 2027, the same timetable as last year.

**Q: Is the pension contribution changing?**
No. The bank continues to match employee contributions up to 8 per cent of salary.

**Q: Can we get paid earlier when salary day falls on a weekend?**
Salaries are already paid on the last working day before a weekend or bank holiday. Grace will make this clearer on the HR portal.

### Learning and development

**Q: Is the learning budget still available?**
Yes. Every permanent member of staff has an annual learning allowance of 600 pounds, plus three learning days. Unused allowance does not carry over.

**Q: Are there more internal moves planned?**
Grace said one in four vacancies this year was filled by an internal candidate. All roles are advertised internally for at least five working days before going outside the bank. A new mentoring scheme opens in October.

**Q: Will there be more technical training for non-technology staff?**
Priya said the technology teams will run open lunchtime sessions on how payments and cards work, starting in November. Recordings will be available on the intranet.

### Wellbeing

**Q: What support is there for people under pressure?**
The employee assistance programme offers free, confidential counselling by phone or video, 24 hours a day. There are now 45 trained mental health first aiders across the bank, and their names are listed on the intranet. Grace encouraged managers to have regular workload conversations and not to send non-urgent messages late at night.

**Q: Will volunteering days continue?**
Yes. All staff can take two paid volunteering days each year.

### Technology and acceptable use

**Q: Can I use my personal phone for work email?**
Hannah explained that personal phones may be used for email and chat only through the approved work app, as set out in the Acceptable Use of Technology policy (POL-002). Work files must not be saved to personal storage.

**Q: Why do I keep getting test phishing emails?**
Hannah said regular practice helps everyone spot suspicious messages. The results are used to plan training and are not used in performance reviews. Staff should report any suspicious email with the report button in their mail client.

### Closing remarks

Grace thanked everyone for their questions. Answers to questions not covered on the day will be posted on the intranet within two weeks.

## Decisions

1. No change to the hybrid working minimum in 2026.
2. Extra bookable desks on the third floor from October.
3. Open lunchtime sessions on payments and cards from November.
