# Cards Product Roadmap Planning

## Meeting details

- Date: 20 August 2026, 13:30 to 15:30
- Chair: Mei Lin Tan
- Purpose: agree the cards roadmap for Q4 2026 and the first half of 2027

## Attendees

- Mei Lin Tan, Cards product owner (chair)
- Elena Popescu, Cards Platform engineering lead
- Arjun Mehta, Fraud analytics lead
- Sofia Marchetti, Enterprise architect
- Daniel Okafor, Head of Payments Operations
- Tomasz Novak, SRE lead

## Agenda

1. Review of the current roadmap
2. Customer feedback and contact centre themes
3. Card controls in Helix Mobile, phase two
4. Virtual cards for online shopping
5. Platform reliability work that competes for capacity
6. Prioritisation

## Discussion

### Review of the current roadmap

Mei Lin went through what was delivered in the first half of 2026. The Helix Everyday Debit Card (PS-002) moved to the new card design with the number printed on the back only. Card freeze and unfreeze from Helix Mobile, part of PS-006, went live in March and is now used by around 9 per cent of active cardholders each month. The Helix Platinum Credit Card (PS-003) travel insurance update slipped to Q4 because of supplier delays.

### Customer feedback and contact centre themes

Daniel shared the top card reasons for calls to the contact centre over the last six months:

1. Card declined, customer does not know why.
2. Lost or stolen card, wanting a replacement faster.
3. Questions about foreign transaction fees on the Platinum card.
4. Online payments declined as suspected fraud.

He noted that declines during the certificate incidents (INC-2025-041 and INC-2026-015) and the fraud threshold change in July (INC-2026-027) caused large call spikes. Customers had no way to see the reason for a decline in the app.

### Card controls in Helix Mobile, phase two

The group discussed the next set of controls for PS-006:

- Turn online, contactless and overseas payments on or off separately.
- Set a personal monthly spending limit with a notification at 80 per cent.
- Show a plain reason for a declined payment in the app, for example "card frozen", "online payments switched off" or "insufficient funds".

Arjun supported decline reasons in principle but warned that fraud-related declines must not reveal the rule that fired. The app should show a general message and offer a quick way to confirm the payment was genuine. Elena said decline reasons need a new field from CardSwitch to the channel layer, which is a medium-sized change.

### Virtual cards for online shopping

Mei Lin proposed a virtual debit card that customers can create in Helix Mobile for online shopping and delete at any time. Sofia said it fits the current CardSwitch design, but card issuance and HSM key handling would need review. Arjun thought virtual cards could reduce card-not-present fraud losses, but wants Sentinel scoring tested on virtual card traffic before launch. The group agreed it is attractive but too large for Q4.

### Platform reliability work that competes for capacity

Elena reminded the group that Cards Platform also owns reliability actions from the payments reliability reviews:

- Certificate expiry monitoring for the secondary CardSwitch site is still waiting on a firewall change. Weekly manual checks continue in the meantime.
- Partial rollout for Sentinel threshold changes, required under the revised POL-005, is being built with Fraud and Security after INC-2026-027.
- A certificate renewal for the Network Gateway is due in September. Elena wants extra engineering time set aside for it, given what happened in April.

Tomasz asked that these items are not traded off against product features. Mei Lin agreed that reliability work is ring-fenced and the roadmap is planned around the remaining capacity.

## Decisions

1. Q4 2026 priorities: separate online, contactless and overseas controls; decline reasons in the app (non-fraud reasons first).
2. Monthly spending limits move to Q1 2027.
3. Virtual cards go to discovery in Q1 2027, with a decision on build in Q2 2027.
4. About 30 per cent of Cards Platform capacity is ring-fenced for reliability work until certificate monitoring and Sentinel partial rollout are complete.
5. The September certificate renewal is treated as a planned change with a named owner and a check on both sites.
