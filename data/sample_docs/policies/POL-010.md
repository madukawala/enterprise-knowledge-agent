# Third-Party and Vendor Risk Management

## Purpose

Helix Commercial Bank depends on third parties for important services, from the FastLane instant payment scheme to card network connectivity and HSM support. When a supplier fails, our customers feel it. This policy sets how we choose, contract, monitor and exit third-party relationships so that the risk they bring is understood and controlled.

## Scope

This policy applies to every third party that provides services, technology or data to the bank, or that can access bank systems or information. It includes payment schemes and clearing houses, software and cloud hosting providers, hardware maintenance firms, consultants and outsourced service providers.

## Vendor tiers

| Tier | Definition | Examples | Review frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Critical | Failure would stop a core banking or payment service, or the vendor holds restricted data. | NorthClear (operator of FastLane), card network connectivity providers, HSM vendor, data centre provider. | Quarterly service review; full risk assessment every year. |
| High | Failure would degrade an important service or the vendor holds confidential data. | Card manufacturing and delivery, notification delivery provider. | Twice a year. |
| Standard | Limited impact on customers or data. | Office supplies, training providers. | Every two years. |

## Policy statements

1. Every third party must have a named business owner and be recorded in the vendor register with its tier.
2. Before contract, every critical and high vendor must pass due diligence covering financial health, information security, resilience, data protection and sub-contractors.
3. Contracts with critical vendors must include service levels, incident notification within one hour for service-affecting incidents, audit rights, data return and exit terms.
4. Critical vendors must have a documented exit plan and a tested way to keep the service running, or degrade it safely, if the vendor fails. For FastLane this is the Instant Payments Gateway degraded mode described in RB-003.
5. Service performance must be measured against agreed service levels and discussed at regular service reviews. Minutes and actions must be recorded.
6. Vendor-caused incidents must be logged under POL-004 with the vendor named in the incident record. Repeated incidents trigger an extraordinary service review.
7. Third-party access to bank systems must follow POL-006 and be removed when the work ends.
8. Contract terms, pricing and service review minutes are confidential under POL-003.
9. Concentration risk must be assessed each year, including where several services rely on the same vendor or the same sub-contractor.
10. Material outsourcing arrangements must be notified to the regulator before they start, through Operational Risk.

## NorthClear and FastLane

NorthClear runs FastLane, the national instant payment scheme, and is our most important critical vendor for payments. Key points of the relationship:

- The scheme timeout is 10 seconds. If FastLane does not respond in time, the payment fails or, with degraded mode enabled, is queued and retried by IPG.
- NorthClear's contracted availability is 99.95% per month, excluding planned maintenance announced at least ten working days in advance.
- NorthClear must notify our Payments Operations team of any scheme problem. At the June 2026 review it committed to doing this within 15 minutes.
- Following INC-2026-012 and the scheme outage in INC-2026-024, an extraordinary service review was held in June 2026 (MTG-006). NorthClear committed to notifying participants within 15 minutes of a scheme problem and to sharing root cause reports within ten working days.

## Exceptions

Where an essential vendor cannot accept a required contract term, the business owner must document the risk and compensating controls. Exceptions for critical vendors need approval from the Head of Operational Risk and Compliance and the CISO.
