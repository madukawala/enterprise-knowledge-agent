# Remote Working Policy

## Purpose

Helix Commercial Bank supports hybrid working. Many of our people can do their jobs well from home for part of the week. This policy explains who can work remotely, what we expect, and how we keep people and information safe when they are not in the office.

We publish this policy so that job applicants know how we work.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees and contractors whose role can be carried out away from a bank office. Some roles need to be on site most or all of the time, for example branch staff, people working in the data centres, and some roles in Payments Operations on peak days.

## Policy statements

### Eligibility and pattern

1. Most office-based staff work a hybrid pattern of at least two days a week in the office, agreed with their line manager.
2. Line managers may set team days when everyone is in the office. Staff should attend these unless they have a good reason.
3. Staff may request a different pattern, including fully remote working, through the flexible working procedure. Requests are considered fairly and answered within two months.
4. Remote working must be done from the United Kingdom unless approved in advance by HR, because of tax, legal and data protection rules. Short periods abroad of up to ten working days a year may be approved by the line manager and HR.

### Working safely

5. Staff working remotely must use a bank-issued laptop. Personal computers must not be used for bank work.
6. Staff must connect through the bank's secure remote access service and sign in with HelixID and multi-factor authentication.
7. Staff must work somewhere private enough that customer or confidential information cannot be seen or overheard. Public places such as cafés and trains are fine for general work but not for customer calls or confidential material.
8. Do not print bank documents at home unless your role has an approved home printer.
9. Home broadband should be reliable. The bank does not pay for home broadband but provides a monthly home-working allowance of GBP 25 through payroll.

### Health and wellbeing

10. Staff must complete the home workstation self-assessment when they start remote working and when their set-up changes. The bank will provide a suitable chair, monitor and keyboard where needed.
11. Working hours are the same as in the office. Staff should take proper breaks and are not expected to answer messages outside their working hours unless they are on call.
12. Line managers must keep in regular contact with remote team members, including at least one one-to-one conversation each month.

### On-call and peak days

13. Staff on an on-call rota must be able to join an incident call within 15 minutes, from wherever they are working.
14. Some teams may ask staff to be on site on peak days such as salary day and month-end. Managers will give at least two weeks' notice.

## Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| HR Director (Grace Mensah) | Owns this policy. |
| HR Systems | Runs the flexible working procedure, allowance payments and self-assessments in PeopleHub. |
| Line managers | Agree working patterns, keep in touch with their team and apply this policy fairly. |
| Fraud and Security | Sets the security requirements for remote access. |
| Employees | Follow this policy, keep information secure and look after their own wellbeing. |

## Exceptions

Line managers may agree short-term changes, for example during a family emergency, without a formal request. Longer-term changes go through the flexible working procedure. Exceptions to the security statements (5 to 8) need approval from Fraud and Security.
