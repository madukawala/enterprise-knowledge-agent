# Payments Operations and Cut-off Times

## Purpose

This policy sets the daily cut-off times for each payment type at Helix Commercial Bank and the operational controls Payments Operations must run each day. Clear cut-off times let us give customers accurate promises about when money will arrive, and let the systems behind payments run their batches in the right order.

## Scope

This policy covers payments processed through PayHub, including:

- instant payments sent through the Instant Payments Gateway (IPG) to FastLane;
- standard and bulk payments;
- standing orders and scheduled payments run by Scheduler;
- payroll files received from PeopleHub and from business customers.

Card payments through CardSwitch are covered by the cards operating procedures and are out of scope.

## Cut-off times

All times are local time on working days unless stated.

| Payment type | Channel | Cut-off | What happens after cut-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instant payment (FastLane) | Helix Mobile, Helix Online | None, available 24/7 | Processed in seconds. If FastLane is unavailable, IPG may queue the payment under RB-003. |
| Standard same-day payment | All channels | 15:30 | Processed next working day. |
| Bulk payments (business) | Helix Online Business, file upload | 14:00 for same-day value | Processed next working day. |
| Payroll files | File upload, PeopleHub | 16:00 one working day before pay day | Treated as late. Payments Operations contacts the customer. |
| Standing orders and scheduled payments | Scheduler | Created or changed by 23:59 the day before | The 06:00 Scheduler run picks up instructions present at 23:59. |
| Payment cancellations | All channels | 23:59 the day before the due date | Cannot be cancelled once released. |

CoreLedger runs the end-of-day batch overnight and must finish by 05:30, so that balances are correct before the 06:00 Scheduler run.

## Policy statements

1. Cut-off times must be shown to customers in Helix Mobile and Helix Online at the point of payment.
2. Changes to cut-off times need approval from the Head of Payments Operations and the Head of Payments Platform, and a change record under POL-005.
3. Payments Operations must check the following every working day: the 06:00 Scheduler run completed; the overnight EOD batch finished by 05:30; the IPG queue is empty or draining; no payments have been in "pending" for over 30 minutes.
4. On peak days (salary day, the last working day of the month and quarter-end), Payments Operations must staff an early shift from 05:30 and an extended shift to 20:00.
5. Before each month-end, Payments Platform must confirm that capacity checks for PayHub and `payhub-db` have been completed.
6. If a payment type is likely to miss its cut-off because of an incident, the incident lead must tell Payments Operations at once. Payments Operations decides whether to extend the cut-off, arrange manual processing or inform customers.
7. Customers who suffer loss because the bank missed a cut-off through its own fault must be refunded any charges and interest under the complaints procedure.
8. Duplicate payments caused by system faults must be identified and reversed within two working days.
9. Large-value manual payments over GBP 250,000 need two authorisers from Payments Operations.

## Exceptions

Payments Operations may extend a cut-off on the day by up to two hours to recover from an incident. Longer extensions need approval from the Head of Payments Operations and must be recorded in the incident report.
