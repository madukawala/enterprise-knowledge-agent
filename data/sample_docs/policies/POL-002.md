# Acceptable Use of Technology

## Purpose

This policy explains what Helix Commercial Bank expects of people when they use the bank's computers, phones, email, messaging tools and internet access. The aim is simple: to let staff work effectively while keeping customers, colleagues and the bank safe.

We publish this policy openly so that candidates, contractors and partners understand our standards before they join us.

## Scope

This policy applies to everyone who uses technology provided by the bank or connects to bank services, including:

- permanent and fixed-term employees;
- contractors, consultants and agency staff;
- anyone using a personal device to access bank email or collaboration tools.

It covers laptops, desktops, mobile phones, tablets, email, instant messaging, video calls, the staff intranet, printers and internet access.

## Policy statements

1. Bank technology is provided for business use. Reasonable personal use, such as a short personal email or checking the news at lunchtime, is allowed as long as it does not interfere with work or break this policy.
2. You must keep your sign-in details private. Never share your password or authentication codes with anyone, including colleagues or people claiming to be from IT support.
3. You must lock your screen whenever you leave your device unattended, even for a moment.
4. You must not install software that has not been approved through the staff software catalogue.
5. You must not connect unapproved storage devices, such as USB sticks, to bank equipment.
6. Bank information must only be stored in approved locations. Do not send bank documents to personal email accounts or upload them to personal file-sharing services.
7. You must not use bank technology to access, create or share material that is offensive, discriminatory, illegal or sexually explicit.
8. Be careful with links and attachments. If an email looks suspicious, report it using the "Report phishing" button and do not click anything in it.
9. When using social media, do not discuss customers, colleagues or confidential bank matters, and make clear that your views are your own.
10. Report lost or stolen devices to the IT Service Desk straight away, at any time of day.
11. The bank monitors use of its systems to protect customers and meet legal duties. Monitoring is carried out lawfully and in proportion to the risk.
12. When you leave the bank, you must return all equipment and must not keep copies of bank information.

## Breaches of this policy

Breaches are taken seriously. Depending on what happened, they may lead to removal of access, disciplinary action under the staff conduct procedure, or, for contractors, ending the engagement. Where a breach may be a crime, the bank may report it to the authorities.

If you make a genuine mistake, such as clicking a phishing link, report it at once. People who report promptly will be supported, not blamed.

## Exceptions

Some roles need tools or access that this policy normally restricts, for example a developer who needs to install a specific tool. Requests go to the IT Service Desk with the line manager's approval. Fraud and Security reviews the request and records any approved exception for a fixed period, normally no longer than 12 months.
