# Card Fraud Response Policy

## Purpose

This policy sets out how Helix Commercial Bank responds when card fraud is reported by a customer or detected by our own systems. It aims to protect customers quickly, limit losses, meet our obligations to refund unauthorised transactions and recover funds where possible.

## Scope

This policy covers fraud on Helix debit and credit cards, including the Helix Everyday Debit Card and the Helix Platinum Credit Card, across:

- card-present transactions;
- card-not-present transactions, such as online and telephone purchases;
- cash withdrawals;
- fraud detected by Sentinel, the fraud screening engine, and fraud reported by customers.

Fraud on instant payments and authorised push payment scams follow the Payments Fraud procedure.

## Policy statements

1. When a customer reports a lost, stolen or compromised card, the card must be blocked immediately. Customers can do this themselves using card controls in Helix Mobile.
2. A replacement card must be issued within three working days unless the customer asks otherwise.
3. Customers must be refunded for unauthorised transactions by the end of the next working day after they report them, unless there are reasonable grounds to suspect the customer acted fraudulently. Those grounds must be recorded and approved by a fraud team leader.
4. Customers are not liable for transactions made after they reported the card as lost or stolen.
5. When Sentinel declines or holds a transaction as suspected fraud, the customer must be notified in Helix Mobile within five minutes and offered a simple way to confirm or reject the transaction.
6. Fraud analysts must work suspected fraud alerts in order of risk. High-risk alerts must be picked up within 15 minutes, 24 hours a day.
7. Chargebacks must be raised with the card network within the network's time limits, and normally within ten working days of the claim.
8. Changes to fraud rules and score thresholds in Sentinel are restricted. They must be tested against recent transaction data, released by canary under POL-005 and approved by the Fraud analytics lead.
9. Where a fraud rule change causes a spike in genuine customers being declined, the change must be rolled back first and investigated afterwards. Payments Operations and the Cards product owner must be told at once.
10. Fraud losses, false-positive rates and customer complaints must be reported monthly to the Fraud and Security leadership and quarterly to Operational Risk.
11. Suspected organised fraud, or fraud involving staff, must be escalated to the CISO and handled with Fraud and Security investigations.
12. Customer data shared with law enforcement must follow the data protection procedure and be approved by Compliance.

## Response targets

| Activity | Target |
|---|---|
| Card block after customer report | Immediate |
| High-risk alert picked up | 15 minutes |
| Customer notification of declined suspected fraud | 5 minutes |
| Refund of unauthorised transactions | End of next working day |
| Replacement card issued | 3 working days |

## Exceptions

Departures from the refund timescale in statement 3 need approval from a fraud team leader and must be recorded with the reason. No exceptions are allowed to statement 1.
