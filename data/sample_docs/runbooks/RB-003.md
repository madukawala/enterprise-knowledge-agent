# FastLane Timeouts and IPG Degraded Mode

## Purpose

The Instant Payments Gateway (IPG) sends instant payments from PayHub to FastLane, the national instant payment scheme run by NorthClear. The scheme gives us 10 seconds to get a response. When NorthClear is slow or unavailable, payments time out and customers see failures.

Until this runbook, IPG had no proper degraded mode. During INC-2026-012 on 16 March 2026, timeouts at NorthClear caused around 4,300 instant payments to fail outright and customers retried many times. This runbook introduces the queue-and-retry degraded mode and explains when to switch it on and off.

## When to use this runbook

- FastLane timeout rate above 5% for more than 5 minutes.
- NorthClear announces an incident or planned maintenance on the scheme bulletin.
- Instant payments are failing but PayHub and the database are healthy.

If the database pool is the cause, use RB-001 instead.

## Symptoms and alerts

| Signal | Source |
|---|---|
| `IpgSchemeTimeoutRate` above 5% | Prometheus, pages Payments Platform on-call |
| p99 scheme response time above 8 seconds | Grafana "IPG and FastLane" |
| Error code `FL-TO-10` in IPG logs | Log search, service `ipg-connector` |
| Customers seeing "transfer could not be completed" in Helix Mobile | Contact centre reports |

## Checks

1. Check the NorthClear participant status page and the scheme bulletin mailbox for announcements.
2. On "IPG and FastLane", compare timeouts against outright rejections. Rejections with a reason code are a different problem (often our routing configuration, see RB-005).
3. Confirm our outbound connection is healthy:
   ```
   kubectl -n ipg exec deploy/ipg-connector -- curl -s -o /dev/null -w "%{http_code} %{time_total}\n" https://fastlane-gw.northclear.example/health
   ```
4. Check whether the problem is limited to one receiving bank. If so, it is usually that bank, not the scheme.
5. Call the NorthClear operations desk to confirm whether they are aware.

## Steps to fix

1. Open an incident and post the first update within 15 minutes, following RB-009.
2. Switch IPG into degraded mode:
   ```
   ipgctl mode set degraded --reason "<incident id>"
   ```
   Degraded mode is switched on by hand for now. In degraded mode IPG:
   - stops sending new payments to FastLane,
   - accepts payments from PayHub and places them on the `ipg.deferred` queue,
   - tells the customer the payment is "accepted and being processed" rather than failed,
   - retries from the queue every 60 seconds with backoff, for up to 60 minutes.
3. Payments still in the queue after 60 minutes are returned to PayHub as failed and the customer is notified. Payments Operations may offer to send high-value payments by the standard payment route.
4. Monitor queue depth on "IPG and FastLane". Raise with NorthClear if depth grows past 5,000.
5. When the timeout rate stays below 1% for 10 minutes, return to normal:
   ```
   ipgctl mode set normal
   ```
   The queue drains automatically at a controlled rate of 50 payments per second.
6. Confirm the queue has emptied and reconcile counts with Payments Operations.
