# Major Incident Communication and Status Page Updates

## Purpose

During a customer-facing incident, people inside and outside the bank need clear and regular updates. This runbook sets out who says what, where and how often. It covers the internal incident channel, the customer status page at status.helixbank.example, the contact centre and senior management.

The technical fix is covered by the runbook for the affected system. This one is about communication only.

## When to use this runbook

- Any SEV1 or SEV2 incident as defined in POL-004.
- A SEV3 that customers can see, for example delayed notifications.
- Planned work that will interrupt Helix Mobile, Helix Online or card payments.

## Symptoms and alerts

There is no specific alert for this runbook. It starts when an incident is declared. Signs that communication is falling behind:

- the contact centre is asking the bridge for information,
- social media mentions of Helix Mobile or card declines are rising,
- senior managers are messaging engineers directly.

## Checks

1. Confirm the severity with the major incident manager.
2. Confirm which services and customer groups are affected (retail, business, card holders).
3. Agree a single incident commander and a separate communications lead. The engineer fixing the problem must not also write updates.

## Steps to fix

1. **Open the incident channel** named `inc-<date>-<short-name>` and pin the bridge link.
2. **First status page post within 15 minutes** of declaring a SEV1 or SEV2. Use the template:
   > We are aware that some customers are having problems with [service]. We are working to fix this and will update this page by [time].
   Do not give a cause or an estimated fix time at this stage.
3. **Update cadence.**

   | Severity | Status page | Internal channel | Senior management |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | SEV1 | every 30 minutes | every 15 minutes | every 30 minutes |
   | SEV2 | every 60 minutes | every 30 minutes | at start and end |
   | SEV3 (customer visible) | at start and end | every 60 minutes | not needed |

4. **Contact centre.** Send the contact centre lead a short script within 20 minutes. Include what customers should do, for example "do not resend payments" or "try another card".
5. **Senior management.** For SEV1, the major incident manager sends an email to the executive on-call list with impact, actions and next update time.
6. **Wording rules.**
   - Plain English, no internal system names.
   - Never blame a third party by name on the status page.
   - Do not promise refunds or compensation; Payments Operations decides this later.
7. **Resolution.** Post "resolved" only after the incident commander confirms 15 minutes of normal service. Leave the post up for 24 hours.
8. **Afterwards.** Save all updates in the incident ticket. Customer-facing wording is reviewed at the post-incident review.
