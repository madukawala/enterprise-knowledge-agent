# Helix Everyday Debit Card

## Overview

The Helix Everyday Debit Card is the standard debit card issued with every Helix personal current account. Customers can use it in shops, online, abroad and at cash machines. Money is taken directly from the customer's current account.

The card is contactless, works with mobile wallets and can be managed in Helix Mobile. It is made from recycled plastic and has the card number printed on the back only, to reduce the risk of the details being copied.

## Customer problem and goals

Customers want a card that:

- just works, wherever they shop;
- is cheap to use abroad;
- they can control themselves, without phoning us;
- is safe if it is lost or stolen.

Our goals are to:

- keep card payments working more than 99.95% of the time;
- let customers freeze, unfreeze and replace a card in the app in under a minute;
- offer clear, low fees for spending abroad.

## Features

- **Contactless payments** up to the contactless limit, with chip and PIN for higher amounts.
- **Mobile wallets**: add the card to a phone or watch wallet straight from Helix Mobile.
- **Online shopping** with secure customer authentication. Customers approve online payments in Helix Mobile when asked.
- **Card controls** in Helix Mobile: freeze and unfreeze, turn off online, contactless or overseas payments, and set a monthly spending limit. More controls are being added over time.
- **View PIN** in the app after biometric sign-in.
- **Instant replacement**: order a new card in the app. A digital card is available in the wallet straight away while the physical card is posted.
- **Spending notifications** for every card payment.
- **Cashback offers** from selected retailers, shown in the app.

## Limits and fees

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Annual card fee | Free |
| Contactless limit per payment | GBP 100 |
| Daily cash withdrawal limit | GBP 500 (customer can lower it in the app) |
| Daily card spending limit | GBP 5,000 |
| Cash withdrawals at Helix and UK cash machines | Free |
| Spending abroad (non-sterling transactions) | 1.5% of the transaction value |
| Cash withdrawals abroad | 1.5% of the amount, minimum GBP 1.50 |
| Replacement card | Free for the first two each year, then GBP 5 |
| Emergency card delivered abroad | GBP 15 |

We do not charge a separate fee for using the card at UK retailers.

## Systems involved

| System | Role |
|---|---|
| CardSwitch | Authorises card payments and cash withdrawals in real time. Runs on a primary and a secondary site. |
| Network Gateway | Connects CardSwitch securely to the card networks. |
| HSMs | Check PINs and card security codes. |
| Sentinel | Screens card payments for fraud. |
| CoreLedger | Holds the account balance and posts card transactions. |
| Helix Mobile | Card controls, view PIN, wallet set-up and notifications. |
| Payment Status Service | Sends spending notifications. |

## Non-functional requirements

- Card authorisation available 24 hours a day with a target of 99.95% per month.
- Authorisation decision returned within 300 milliseconds for 99% of transactions, measured at CardSwitch.
- Card processing must continue if one CardSwitch site fails.
- Card data is protected to the card industry security standard. Full card numbers are never shown in Helix Mobile, except to the cardholder after biometric sign-in.
- Spending notifications sent within 30 seconds of authorisation.
