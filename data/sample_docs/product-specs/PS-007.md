# Real-Time Payment Notifications

## Overview

Real-Time Payment Notifications tells customers what is happening to their payments as it happens. When a customer sends money, receives money, or a payment fails, they get a push notification in Helix Mobile and the payment status in the app updates straight away.

Notifications are produced by the Payment Status Service (PSS), which reads payment events from the Kafka topic `payments.status`.

## Customer problem and goals

Complaints analysis for the first half of 2025 showed:

- the most common payment complaint was "I don't know if my payment went through";
- when a status stayed at "pending", some customers sent the payment again, leading to duplicates and refund requests;
- customers expecting money had no way of knowing it had arrived without checking their balance.

Goals:

- every outgoing payment shows a final status (sent, failed or returned) within 30 seconds for instant payments;
- customers are told about incoming payments within 30 seconds;
- reduce "where is my payment" contacts by 25%;
- reduce duplicate payments caused by customers retrying.

## Features

- **Payment sent**: confirmation when an outgoing payment completes, with amount, payee and reference.
- **Payment failed**: a clear reason and what to do next, such as "The receiving bank could not accept this payment. Your money has not left your account."
- **Payment received**: notification for incoming payments above a customer-chosen amount, default GBP 1.
- **Standing order failed**: notification when a scheduled payment cannot be made.
- **Status in the app**: payment status updates on screen without refreshing.
- **Preferences**: customers choose which notifications they receive. Failure notifications are always on.
- **Quiet hours**: customers can mute non-urgent notifications overnight. Failure notifications still come through.

## Limits and fees

Notifications are free. Customers can receive up to 200 notifications per day; above this, received-payment notifications are combined into a daily summary.

## Systems involved

| System | Role |
|---|---|
| PayHub | Publishes events when payments are created, sent, completed or rejected. |
| Instant Payments Gateway | Publishes FastLane responses as events. |
| Scheduler | Publishes standing order results. |
| Kafka | Topic `payments.status` carries all payment status events, partitioned by account. |
| Payment Status Service | Consumes events, updates payment status, decides which notifications to send. |
| Notification delivery provider | Third-party service that delivers push notifications to devices. |
| Helix Mobile | Shows notifications and live status. |

## Non-functional requirements

| Requirement | Target |
|---|---|
| Event to notification time | 95% within 30 seconds, 99% within 2 minutes |
| Consumer lag on `payments.status` | Alert if above 10,000 messages or 2 minutes |
| Peak throughput | 1,500 events per second (salary day and month-end) |
| Availability | 99.9% per month |
| Ordering | Events for the same payment processed in order |
| Duplicates | Each event processed once; repeated events must not send repeated notifications |

Payments themselves must not depend on PSS: if PSS is slow, payments still complete, but statuses and notifications are delayed. PSS runs a fixed number of consumer instances sized for peak days; the team will review whether automatic scaling is needed after the first salary day in production.

## Open questions

1. How long should a payment show "pending" before the app warns the customer not to resend it?
2. Should we fall back to text messages for failure notifications when a customer has push notifications switched off?
3. What is the right number of partitions on `payments.status` for expected growth over the next two years?
4. Who owns the relationship with the notification delivery provider, and what vendor risk tier should it have?
5. If a status is delayed, customers may resend a payment. Should PayHub require an idempotency key on every payment request so that a repeated request is rejected? This needs changes in both apps.
