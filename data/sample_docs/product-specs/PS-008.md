# Fraud Scoring Rules for Card-Not-Present Payments

## Overview

This specification describes the scoring model and rule set Sentinel uses to screen card-not-present (CNP) payments on Helix debit and credit cards. CNP covers online, in-app and telephone purchases, where the card is not physically present.

This document is restricted under POL-003. It contains score thresholds and rule names. Do not copy any part of it into tickets, chat or presentations.

## Customer problem and goals

CNP fraud makes up about 70% of our card fraud losses by value. At the same time, every genuine payment we decline frustrates a customer and may lose us the relationship. The rule set must balance these two things.

Goals for 2026:

- reduce CNP fraud losses by 20% against 2025;
- keep the false-positive rate (genuine payments declined or challenged) below 1 in 400 CNP transactions;
- keep Sentinel's added time on card authorisation below 50 milliseconds at the 99th percentile.

## Features

### Risk score

Sentinel calculates a score from 0 to 1,000 for every CNP authorisation. The score combines a machine-learned model with rule adjustments. Inputs include device and channel signals, merchant category, transaction amount compared with the customer's history, time since the card was added to a wallet, and recent card control changes.

### Score bands and actions

These bands replace the two-band set described in ARC-007 (step-up from 620, decline from 850). The January 2026 recalibration added a separate very-high band and lowered the step-up point, because fraud analytics found most confirmed fraud scoring between 550 and 620. The CardSwitch fallback limit was also lowered from GBP 150 to GBP 100 at the same time.

| Band | Score | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Low | 0 to 549 | Approve. |
| Medium | 550 to 749 | Approve if strong customer authentication succeeds in Helix Mobile; otherwise decline. |
| High | 750 to 879 | Decline and send a "Was this you?" notification. Customer can confirm and retry. |
| Very high | 880 to 1,000 | Decline, block the card for CNP and create a high-risk alert for fraud operations. |

### Named rules

| Rule | Logic | Score adjustment |
|---|---|---|
| CNP-VEL-01 | More than 5 CNP attempts on the same card within 10 minutes | +200 |
| CNP-VEL-02 | More than 3 different merchants within 30 minutes, each over GBP 100 | +150 |
| CNP-NEWDEV-03 | Card used from a device never seen for this customer, and amount over GBP 250 | +120 |
| CNP-GEO-04 | Merchant country differs from the customer's device location and the overseas control is on | +180 |
| CNP-HRMCC-05 | High-risk merchant category (gift cards, crypto exchange, money transfer) | +100 |
| CNP-TRUST-06 | Merchant where the customer has paid successfully at least 3 times in 90 days | -150 |
| CNP-WALLET-07 | Wallet provisioned less than 24 hours ago and first CNP payment over GBP 500 | +220 |

### Rule changes

Threshold and rule changes are configuration changes under POL-005. Each change must be:

1. back-tested on the last 30 days of CNP transactions, showing expected change in fraud caught and false positives;
2. approved by the Fraud analytics lead (Arjun Mehta);
3. released by canary to no more than 5% of CNP traffic for at least 2 hours, with the decline rate watched against the previous week;
4. rolled back at once if the CNP decline rate rises by more than 1.5 percentage points over the baseline.

## Limits and fees

Not applicable. Customers are not charged for fraud screening.

## Systems involved

| System | Role |
|---|---|
| CardSwitch | Sends CNP authorisation requests to Sentinel and applies the decision. |
| Sentinel | Calculates the score and applies the rules and band actions. |
| Card controls service | Supplies control settings used by CNP-GEO-04. |
| Helix Mobile | Strong customer authentication and "Was this you?" notifications. |
| Fraud case management | Receives very-high alerts for fraud operations. |

## Non-functional requirements

- Sentinel decision within 50 milliseconds at the 99th percentile.
- If Sentinel does not respond within 150 milliseconds, CardSwitch applies the fallback: approve CNP payments up to GBP 100, decline above that.
- Every score, rule hit and decision logged for 13 months for model tuning and disputes.
- Rule configuration held in version control; only the Fraud analytics team can approve merges.

## Open questions

1. Should the medium band threshold move from 550 to 600 to reduce authentication challenges at peak shopping times?
2. CNP-HRMCC-05 is producing a high false-positive rate on gift card purchases in December. Should it be suspended for known large retailers?
3. Should the fallback limit of GBP 100 be lowered for cards with recent high-band scores?
