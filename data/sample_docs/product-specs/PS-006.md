# Card Controls in Helix Mobile

## Overview

Card Controls gives customers direct control over how their Helix debit and credit cards can be used, from inside Helix Mobile. Customers can freeze a card, switch off particular types of payment and set their own spending limits. Changes take effect within seconds because they are applied by CardSwitch at authorisation time.

This specification covers release 2 of Card Controls, which adds merchant category blocks and spending limits to the freeze feature launched last year.

## Customer problem and goals

Calls to the card services team show that:

- around 30% of "lost card" calls end with the customer finding the card again within a day;
- customers want to stop certain spending, such as gambling, without cancelling the card;
- parents and carers want to limit spending on cards used by people they support.

Goals:

- reduce lost and stolen card calls by 40% by letting customers freeze a card themselves;
- give customers who want it a reliable way to block gambling spending;
- reduce fraud losses by letting customers switch off payment types they never use.

## Features

| Control | Description | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| Freeze card | Declines all new transactions except recurring payments the customer has chosen to allow. Unfreeze at any time. | Debit and credit |
| Online payments | Switch card-not-present payments on or off. | Debit and credit |
| Contactless | Switch contactless payments on or off. | Debit and credit |
| Overseas payments | Switch off all payments made outside the UK. | Debit and credit |
| Cash withdrawals | Switch off cash machine withdrawals. | Debit and credit |
| Gambling block | Blocks gambling merchant categories. Once switched on, it can only be switched off after a 48-hour cooling-off period. | Debit and credit |
| Monthly spending limit | Customer sets a monthly limit. Transactions that would exceed it are declined. | Debit only in release 2 |
| Report lost or stolen | Cancels the card permanently and orders a replacement. | Debit and credit |

When a transaction is declined because of a control, the customer gets a push notification explaining which control caused it, with a button to change it.

## Limits and fees

- All card controls are free.
- Monthly spending limit can be set between GBP 50 and GBP 10,000 in steps of GBP 50.
- A customer can change controls as often as they like, except the gambling block cooling-off period.

## Systems involved

| System | Role |
|---|---|
| Helix Mobile | Screens for viewing and changing controls. |
| Edge Gateway | Authenticates and routes control changes. |
| Card controls service | New service owned by Cards Platform. Stores each card's control settings and publishes changes. |
| CardSwitch | Reads the control settings during authorisation and declines transactions that break them, before calling Sentinel. Settings are replicated to both the primary and secondary sites. |
| Payment Status Service | Sends decline notifications. |
| CoreLedger | Provides month-to-date spend for the spending limit check, refreshed in real time from card authorisations. |

## Non-functional requirements

- A control change must take effect at both CardSwitch sites within five seconds for 99% of changes.
- Control checks must add no more than 10 milliseconds to card authorisation time.
- If the card controls service is unavailable, CardSwitch uses the last known settings held locally. It must never fail open for a frozen card.
- Every change is audited with the time, channel and device.
- Screens meet accessibility standards, including screen reader support.
