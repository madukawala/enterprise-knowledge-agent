# Helix Platinum Credit Card

## Overview

The Helix Platinum Credit Card is our premium credit card for personal customers. It offers a long interest-free period on purchases, rewards on everyday spending and no fees for spending abroad. It is aimed at customers who pay their balance regularly and travel often.

Customers can apply in Helix Mobile or Helix Online and get a decision in minutes. Approved customers can start using a digital card in their wallet straight away.

## Customer problem and goals

Customers told us that credit cards are often confusing, with hidden charges and complex rewards. They want:

- clear interest rates and fees;
- rewards that are easy to understand and use;
- no extra charges when travelling;
- tools to help them stay in control of their borrowing.

Our goals are to:

- give a credit decision within five minutes for most applicants;
- keep all fees simple and shown on a single page;
- help customers avoid persistent debt with timely reminders.

## Features

### Rewards

- 0.5% cashback on all eligible purchases, paid monthly to the card account.
- 1% cashback on travel and fuel purchases.
- Cashback is capped at GBP 300 a year.

### Interest-free periods

- 0% on purchases for the first 15 months from account opening.
- 0% on balance transfers for the first 12 months, made within 60 days of opening.
- Up to 56 days interest-free on purchases after the introductory period, if the full balance is paid every month.

### Travel benefits

- No fee on spending abroad.
- Travel accident and delayed baggage cover when travel is paid with the card.

### Staying in control

- Choose a payment date that suits you.
- Set up a direct debit for the minimum, a fixed amount or the full balance.
- Reminders before the payment is due and when close to the credit limit.
- Card controls in Helix Mobile, including freezing the card and blocking gambling transactions, with more controls being added over time.

## Limits and fees

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Annual fee | GBP 0 in the first year, then GBP 60 a year (waived if annual spend exceeds GBP 6,000) |
| Representative APR | 24.9% APR (variable) |
| Purchase rate after introductory period | 24.9% a year (variable) |
| Balance transfer fee | 3% of the amount transferred, minimum GBP 5 |
| Cash advance fee | 3% of the amount, minimum GBP 3 |
| Cash advance interest rate | 29.9% a year (variable) |
| Late payment fee | GBP 12 |
| Over-limit fee | None |
| Spending abroad fee | None |
| Credit limit | GBP 1,000 to GBP 20,000, subject to status |
| Minimum payment | 1% of the balance plus interest and fees, or GBP 25, whichever is higher |

All credit is subject to status. Representative example: assumed credit limit of GBP 1,200 at a purchase rate of 24.9% a year (variable) gives a representative 24.9% APR (variable).

## Systems involved

| System | Role |
|---|---|
| Helix Mobile and Helix Online | Application journey, card management and statements. |
| CardSwitch | Authorises card transactions. |
| Sentinel | Screens transactions for fraud. |
| CoreLedger | Holds the credit card account, calculates interest and produces statements in the end-of-day batch. |
| Payment Status Service | Sends payment reminders and spending notifications. |

## Non-functional requirements

- Credit decision returned within five minutes for at least 85% of applications.
- Card authorisation availability and speed as for the debit card: 99.95% availability and decisions within 300 milliseconds.
- Interest and cashback calculated daily in the CoreLedger end-of-day batch and shown in the app by 07:00.
- All customer-facing documents meet consumer credit disclosure rules.
