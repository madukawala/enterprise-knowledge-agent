# Helix Instant Transfer

## Overview

Helix Instant Transfer lets personal and business customers send money from their Helix current account to an account at another UK bank in seconds, at any time of day, every day of the year. Payments are sent through FastLane, the national instant payment scheme.

The service is available in Helix Mobile and Helix Online. It replaces the older "faster transfer" option, which only worked during extended business hours.

## Customer problem and goals

Customers told us that:

- they did not know when a transfer would arrive, especially in the evening and at weekends;
- they wanted proof that the money had reached the other person;
- they worried about sending money to the wrong account.

Our goals are to:

- deliver most transfers to the receiving bank within ten seconds;
- show a clear status for every payment: sent, received or failed;
- reduce misdirected payments by checking the payee's name before the money leaves.

## Features

### Sending a transfer

- Pay a saved payee or a new payee using sort code and account number.
- Add a reference of up to 18 characters.
- See the payee's name check result before confirming: "match", "close match" or "no match".
- Confirm the payment with biometrics or the app passcode in Helix Mobile, or with a security code in Helix Online.

### Payment status

- The payment shows "Sent" once the receiving bank has accepted it.
- If the receiving bank or the scheme is temporarily unavailable, the payment may show "Processing". We will keep trying for a short period and tell the customer as soon as it completes or fails.
- Customers receive a notification when the payment completes, if they have notifications switched on.

### Safety

- New payees above a set amount are held for extra checks.
- Customers can see and download a receipt for each transfer.
- Payments cannot be recalled once sent, so the app reminds customers to check the details.

## Limits and fees

| Item | Personal customers | Business customers |
|---|---|---|
| Fee per transfer | Free | Free on Business Everyday; GBP 0.20 on Business Plus after the first 100 a month |
| Maximum single transfer | GBP 25,000 | GBP 100,000 |
| Daily limit | GBP 25,000 | GBP 250,000 |
| First payment to a new payee | Up to GBP 5,000 straight away; higher amounts may be held for checks | Up to GBP 20,000 straight away |
| Minimum transfer | GBP 0.01 | GBP 0.01 |

Transfers above these limits can be made as standard payments or by contacting us.

## Systems involved

| System | Role |
|---|---|
| Helix Mobile and Helix Online | Customer journey for setting up and confirming the transfer. |
| Edge Gateway | Routes and protects requests from the channels. |
| PayHub | Creates and orchestrates the payment. |
| Instant Payments Gateway (IPG) | Sends the payment to FastLane and handles responses. |
| FastLane | The national instant payment scheme operated by NorthClear. |
| Sentinel | Screens the payment for fraud before it is sent. |
| CoreLedger | Debits the customer's account. |
| Payment Status Service | Updates the status and sends the completion notification. |

## Non-functional requirements

- Available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, with a target of 99.9% per month, excluding scheme outages.
- 95% of transfers confirmed to the customer within 10 seconds.
- Each transfer carries a unique reference so that repeated taps cannot create duplicate payments.
- Meets accessibility standards for both apps.
- Customer data and payment details encrypted in transit and at rest.
