# Bulk Payments for Business Customers

## Overview

Bulk Payments lets business customers pay many people in one go, for example staff salaries, supplier invoices or expense claims. Customers upload a file or build a payment list in Helix Online Business, approve it, and PayHub processes each payment individually.

This specification covers the redesigned bulk payments service, which moves bulk processing from the legacy file gateway onto PayHub.

## Customer problem and goals

Our 48,000 business customers include many small firms who pay their staff and suppliers in batches. Feedback from relationship managers showed that:

- the old file format was hard to produce and errors were only found after upload;
- customers could not see which individual payments in a batch had failed;
- there was no way to have two people approve a large batch.

Goals:

- validate files on upload and show errors line by line before submission;
- give a status for every payment in the batch;
- support dual approval for batches above a customer-set amount;
- process a 10,000-line payroll file in under 15 minutes.

## Features

- **File upload** in CSV or the standard bulk payment format. Up to 20,000 payments per file.
- **Manual batches** built from saved payees in Helix Online Business.
- **Validation on upload**: sort code and account checks, duplicate line detection, total amount check against the customer's control total.
- **Approval rules**: single or dual approval, set by the business administrator, with optional amount thresholds.
- **Scheduling**: choose a payment date up to 31 days ahead.
- **Payment method**: each line is sent as an instant payment via FastLane where possible, or as a standard payment if the amount is above the instant limit.
- **Batch tracking**: dashboard showing each batch and each line as pending, sent, failed or returned. Failed lines can be corrected and resubmitted without re-sending the whole batch.
- **Reports**: downloadable batch report for reconciliation.

## Limits and fees

| Item | Business Everyday | Business Plus |
|---|---|---|
| Fee per batch | GBP 2.00 | Free |
| Fee per payment line | GBP 0.15 | GBP 0.10 after the first 200 a month |
| Maximum lines per file | 5,000 | 20,000 |
| Maximum batch value | GBP 500,000 | GBP 5,000,000 |
| Same-day cut-off | 14:00 | 14:00 |

Payroll files must be received by 16:00 one working day before pay day, in line with POL-007.

## Systems involved

| System | Role |
|---|---|
| Helix Online Business | Upload, approval and tracking screens. |
| Edge Gateway | Authenticates and routes requests. |
| PayHub | Validates the file, splits it into individual payment instructions stored in `payment_instructions` in `payhub-db`, and orchestrates processing. |
| Instant Payments Gateway | Sends lines that go by FastLane. |
| CoreLedger | Debits the business account, either per line or as a single bulk debit depending on customer preference. |
| Payment Status Service | Updates line statuses and notifies the approver when the batch completes. |
| Sentinel | Screens unusual batches, such as first-time payees with high values. |

## Non-functional requirements

- A 10,000-line file processed within 15 minutes of approval, including on salary day and month-end.
- PayHub must handle at least three times the average daily bulk volume on the last working day of the month.
- No duplicate payments if a customer resubmits the same file within 24 hours; the duplicate file is flagged for confirmation.
- Batch data kept for seven years.
- Availability 99.9% in business hours (07:00 to 20:00 on working days).
