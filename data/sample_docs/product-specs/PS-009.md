# Savings Account Interest Calculation

## Overview

This specification describes how CoreLedger calculates, accrues and pays interest on Helix savings accounts. It applies to the Easy Access Saver, the 95-Day Notice Saver and the Regular Saver.

It is confidential because it includes rate-setting rules and details of the end-of-day calculation that are not published to customers.

## Customer problem and goals

Customers expect interest to be correct to the penny and paid on time. Complaints and audit findings in 2024 showed:

- interest shown in the app sometimes differed from the amount paid, because the app estimate used a different method;
- rate changes were applied on different days for different products;
- interest for accounts opened or closed mid-month was hard to explain.

Goals:

- one calculation method, used by CoreLedger and shown in the app;
- daily accrual visible to customers by 07:00;
- rate changes applied on the effective date for every account, with the correct notice given.

## Features

### Calculation method

- Interest is calculated daily on the end-of-day cleared balance.
- Daily interest = cleared balance x annual rate / 365 (366 in a leap year).
- Daily interest is accrued to six decimal places and rounded to the nearest penny only when paid.
- Tiered rates apply to the whole balance at the rate of the tier reached, not to slices of the balance.

### Products

| Product | Rate (AER, variable) | Paid | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Easy Access Saver | 3.10% on balances up to GBP 250,000 | Monthly, on the first working day | Unlimited withdrawals. |
| 95-Day Notice Saver | 3.85% | Monthly | Withdrawals need 95 days' notice. Withdrawals without notice lose 95 days' interest. |
| Regular Saver | 5.00% fixed for 12 months | On maturity | Deposits from GBP 25 to GBP 300 per month. One missed month is allowed. |

### Rate changes

- Rate reductions on variable products need 14 days' notice to customers before the effective date.
- Rate increases may be applied immediately.
- Rate changes are loaded into CoreLedger as dated rate tables at least two working days before the effective date, with dual approval from Treasury and Core Banking.
- The rate table change is a configuration change and follows POL-005.

### Interest payment

- Accrued interest is posted to the account in the end-of-day batch on the payment date.
- Interest is paid gross. No tax is deducted.
- On account closure, accrued interest is paid up to the day before closure.

## Limits and fees

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Minimum to open | GBP 1 (Regular Saver: GBP 25) |
| Maximum balance | GBP 1,000,000 per customer per product |
| Account fees | None |
| Early withdrawal charge (Notice Saver) | 95 days' interest on the amount withdrawn |

## Systems involved

| System | Role |
|---|---|
| CoreLedger | Holds balances and rate tables, calculates and accrues interest in the end-of-day batch, posts interest. |
| Helix Mobile and Helix Online | Show the rate, accrued interest to date and projected interest. |
| Treasury rate tool | Where rates are proposed and approved before loading into CoreLedger. |
| Statements service | Produces annual interest statements for tax purposes. |

## Non-functional requirements

- The interest step of the end-of-day batch must complete within 45 minutes for 3.5 million savings accounts, so that the whole batch finishes by 05:30.
- Interest calculations must be reproducible: given the balance history and rate tables, a rerun must give the same answer to six decimal places.
- Every rate table change is audited with approver names and times.
- Interest shown in the app must equal CoreLedger's accrued figure.

## Open questions

1. Should we move to paying interest on the anniversary of account opening for the Regular Saver, to spread the load on maturity dates?
2. The interest step has grown from 28 to 39 minutes over the past year. Do we need to split it across more batch workers before the end-of-day window becomes tight?
3. Should a bonus rate for new customers be supported, and how would it be shown in the app?
