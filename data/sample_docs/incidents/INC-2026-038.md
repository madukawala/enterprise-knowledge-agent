# Intermittent FastLane timeouts

## Summary

On Tuesday 6 October 2026, between 14:10 and 15:25, a share of instant payment requests to FastLane took longer than the 10-second scheme timeout. NorthClear later confirmed a faulty network link between their two data centres.

IPG degraded mode, described in RB-003, switched on automatically at 14:12. Payments that timed out were queued and retried, and customers saw "payment processing" rather than "payment failed". Almost all payments went through with a short delay. Only 86 payments failed.

This is the third FastLane / NorthClear incident in the last year, after INC-2026-012 and INC-2026-024. Unlike those incidents, the impact on customers was small, and it was kept at SEV3. It shows the value of the degraded mode built after INC-2026-012 and the retry window extended from 60 minutes to 3 hours after INC-2026-024.

## Root cause

The trigger was a faulty network link between NorthClear's two data centres, which caused some FastLane requests to be routed slowly. NorthClear replaced the link at 15:20.

Nothing failed on the bank's side. IPG behaved as designed in RB-003.

**Cause category:** FastLane / NorthClear third-party timeouts (third occurrence, after INC-2026-012 and INC-2026-024). Degraded mode limited the impact.

## Resolution

No action was needed beyond monitoring the queue. Payments Operations checked the 86 failed payments and confirmed that the customers had been told and not debited.

## Lessons learned

- Degraded mode turned what would have been a SEV2 like INC-2026-012 into a minor event. The automatic trigger meant no one had to make a decision under pressure.
- NorthClear met its new 15-minute notification commitment agreed at the June vendor review (MTG-006).
- FastLane problems are likely to keep happening. Our aim is to keep them from becoming customer outages, and this incident shows that is now working.
