# Month-end payment failures in PayHub

## Summary

On Friday 29 May 2026, the last working day of the month, the PayHub database connection pool was exhausted again between 14:45 and 16:20. Payment submissions from Helix Mobile, Helix Online and business bulk files failed or were delayed for 1 hour 35 minutes.

This is the third connection pool outage this year, after INC-2026-003 (salary day, January) and INC-2026-009 (month-end, February), with an early warning in INC-2025-031. The indexes added after those incidents were in place and a load test had been run, but the load test used April volumes. May month-end volume was 22% higher because of new business customers onboarded to bulk payments. RB-001 was followed and diagnosis was much faster than in earlier incidents.

## Root cause

Unlike the earlier incidents, there was no new slow query. The indexes added after INC-2026-003 and INC-2026-009 were working. The problem was plain volume: 22% more bulk payment lines than April, plus normal month-end retail traffic, needed more than 200 connections at the peak.

The pre-month-end load test agreed after INC-2026-009 was run on 26 May, but it replayed April traffic. It passed with peak pool usage of 88%, which should itself have been treated as a warning.

A wider design issue was also noted. Read-only work, such as status lookups and Payments Operations reports, uses the same pool and primary database as payment writes.

**Cause category:** PayHub database connection pool exhaustion at peak (third occurrence, after INC-2026-003 and INC-2026-009).

## Resolution

The pool was raised to 300 during the incident, and to 350 per pod group on 2 June after a load test at 130% of May volume. Database CPU and memory headroom was confirmed by the database team.

## Lessons learned

- RB-001 cut diagnosis time from over an hour in INC-2026-003 to about 20 minutes.
- A load test is only as good as the volumes it uses. Business growth must feed into the test.
- Raising the pool buys time but does not solve the design problem. Read and write traffic need to be separated.
