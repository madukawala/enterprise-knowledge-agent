# FastLane scheme outage stopped instant payments

## Summary

On Thursday 11 June 2026 the FastLane instant payment scheme, run by NorthClear, was unavailable from 09:12 to 11:20. NorthClear's primary data centre had a storage failure and their failover to the second site did not work. No bank could send or receive instant payments during this time.

The IPG degraded mode added after INC-2026-012 and described in RB-003 had become automatic at the end of May 2026. It switched on by itself at 09:14 and started queuing payments. However, degraded mode holds a payment for at most 60 minutes before giving up and returning it to the customer. The outage lasted just over two hours, so many queued payments were returned unpaid.

This is the second FastLane / NorthClear incident in the last year, after INC-2026-012, and the most serious.

## Impact

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| FastLane unavailable | 09:12 to 11:20 (2 hours 8 minutes) |
| Payments queued by degraded mode | about 52,600 |
| Queued payments returned after the 60-minute retry window | about 33,700, value £22.9 million |
| Queued payments sent after recovery | about 18,900, value £12.4 million, all settled by 11:55 |
| Customers affected by returned payments | about 27,800 |
| Complaints | 3,950 |

Customers whose payment was returned got a notification saying it could not be sent, with an option to send it as a standard next-day payment. No customer was debited for a returned payment.

## Root cause

The trigger was a storage failure at NorthClear and a failed failover on their side. NorthClear's incident report, received on 16 June, confirmed that their failover had not been tested in the previous 9 months.

On our side, degraded mode was designed around the slowdown seen in INC-2026-012, which lasted under 90 minutes with partial service. The 60-minute retry window was chosen to keep customers from waiting too long for an answer. It was never tested against a full outage lasting longer than that, and the window was fixed in code rather than set in configuration.

**Cause category:** FastLane / NorthClear third-party timeouts and outage (second occurrence, after INC-2026-012).

## Resolution

We waited for NorthClear to recover. Degraded mode protected the payments made in the last hour of the outage. The retry window could not be extended during the incident because it was fixed in code.

## Lessons learned

- Automatic degraded mode worked. It switched on two minutes after the outage started, without anyone touching it.
- The design assumed short slowdowns. We now plan for full outages too.
- Settings we may need to change during an incident must be configuration, not code.
- Vendor oversight under POL-010 needs evidence of resilience, not assurances.
