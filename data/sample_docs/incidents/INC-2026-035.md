# Card payments declined after renewed certificate not deployed

## Summary

On Friday 18 September 2026 the mTLS client certificate used by the secondary CardSwitch site (data centre NB2) to connect to the Network Gateway expired at 06:00. The certificate had been renewed on 21 August, but the renewed certificate was only deployed to the primary site (NB1). From 06:00 to 08:05 card authorisations through the secondary site failed. As the secondary site carries about 40% of traffic, around two in five card payments were declined.

This is the third certificate-related incident since INC-2025-041, after INC-2026-015. Expiry monitoring went live in March 2026 and was extended to the full chain after INC-2026-015, but only on the primary site. Checks on the secondary site were blocked by a firewall change and had not been finished. The monitoring therefore saw a valid, renewed certificate and raised no alert.

## Root cause

RB-002 says the certificate must be installed on both sites. The engineer who renewed it in August installed it on the primary site, tested it and closed the ticket, planning to do the secondary site the next day. That second step was never done.

The deeper cause is that the actions from the two earlier incidents were never fully completed:

- After INC-2025-041 we promised automated monitoring. It went live in March 2026 for the primary leaf certificate only.
- After INC-2026-015 it was extended to the full chain on the primary site. The secondary site was left waiting on a firewall change.
- After INC-2026-015 we promised a single pipeline to deploy to both sites. It was overdue.

**Cause category:** expired or mis-deployed mTLS certificate between CardSwitch and the Network Gateway (third occurrence, after INC-2025-041 and INC-2026-015).

## Resolution

Traffic was moved to the primary site, the renewed certificate was deployed to the secondary site and traffic was rebalanced.

Elena Popescu and Tomasz Novak were given joint ownership of closing the certificate actions. By 9 October 2026:

- The firewall change was made and monitoring now probes the live endpoints on both sites, checking expiry and the full chain.
- Certificates are deployed to both sites by one pipeline job, which fails unless both sites pass a test handshake.

Certificate expiry monitoring is now fully in place.

## Lessons learned

- Three incidents with the same cause in under a year came from partly finished actions. "Partly done" must not be treated as done.
- Monitoring must check what is live on every site, not what we think was deployed.
