# Card-not-present payments declined after fraud threshold change

## Summary

On Wednesday 8 July 2026 a change to a decline threshold in Sentinel, the fraud screening engine, was made directly in production at 13:05. The new value was far stricter than intended, and Sentinel started declining a large share of genuine online card payments. The threshold was restored at 15:40.

This is the second configuration incident in the last year, after INC-2026-007. The Change Management Policy (POL-005) revision approved in the week of 16 April 2026 requires a canary release (5% of traffic for 30 minutes) and a rollback plan for production configuration changes. Sentinel rule changes were treated as business rule changes made in the Sentinel console, so they were not seen as in scope.

Details of the rule itself are restricted and are held in PS-008 and the Fraud and Security change record.

## Impact

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Duration of customer impact | 13:05 to 15:40 (2 hours 35 minutes) |
| Card-not-present payments declined | about 23,600 |
| Customers affected | about 17,900 |
| Value of declined transactions | about £2.1 million |
| Fraud prevented in the same period | no confirmed fraud cases among the declines |
| Complaints | 940 |

In-store card payments and cash withdrawals were not affected.

## Root cause

Arjun Mehta's team planned to tighten one rule group after a rise in online fraud. The approved value was entered with an extra digit missing, so the threshold was set far too low and many more payments scored above it. The change was made straight in production. It was not run in shadow mode or on a small share of traffic, and there was no rollback plan. The console has no version history or one-step undo. The old value was only in the audit log, which only six named staff can read, so finding it took 40 minutes.

Card decline alerts are tuned for CardSwitch errors. Sentinel declines came through as normal "do not honour" responses and took 47 minutes to page.

**Cause category:** configuration change deployed without a canary (second occurrence, after INC-2026-007).

## Resolution

The threshold was restored to its previous value, then the intended new value was applied on 10 July in shadow mode for 24 hours and confirmed before going live.

## Lessons learned

- A policy change only works if everyone agrees what counts as a configuration change. Sentinel thresholds are configuration.
- Every system that can change production behaviour needs version history and a fast way back. RB-005 assumes the old value is known.
- Shadow mode lets fraud rules be tested on real traffic with no customer impact. It should be the default.
- A later scheduler incident, INC-2026-043, shows the gap was not closed in every system.
