# FastLane timeouts caused failed instant payments

## Summary

On Monday 16 March 2026 NorthClear, which runs the FastLane instant payment scheme, had a database problem that made FastLane respond very slowly. Between 12:05 and 13:30 many instant payments sent by the Instant Payments Gateway (IPG) took longer than the 10-second scheme timeout and were marked as failed. IPG had no degraded mode, so every timeout became a failed payment shown to the customer.

This is the first third-party timeout incident with FastLane in the last year. The queue-and-retry degraded mode in RB-003 was designed and written as a follow-up to this incident.

## Root cause

The trigger was outside the bank. NorthClear told us on 18 March that a database index rebuild on their side ran during business hours by mistake.

The reason it hurt customers so much was on our side. IPG treated every timeout as a final failure. It did not hold the payment and try again when FastLane recovered, and it did not tell the customer the payment was still being processed. In 312 cases the payment had in fact reached FastLane and settled even though IPG had timed out, which left the customer unsure whether they had paid. Some paid again.

**Cause category:** FastLane / NorthClear third-party timeouts with no degraded mode in IPG (first occurrence).

## Resolution

No fix was possible on our side during the incident. FastLane recovered on its own. Payments Operations reconciled payments with unknown outcome against the FastLane settlement report and returned 47 duplicate payments to customers.

## Lessons learned

- We cannot control FastLane, but we can control how IPG behaves when FastLane is slow.
- Telling a customer a payment failed when we do not know is worse than telling them it is in progress. It leads to duplicate payments.
- NorthClear's communication during the incident was slow. Our vendor contract should set clear expectations.
