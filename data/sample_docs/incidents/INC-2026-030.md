# Delayed payment notifications and failed status updates

## Summary

On Friday 14 August 2026 the Payment Status Service (PSS) fell behind on the Kafka topic `payments.status` between 16:30 and 18:05. Payment statuses in Helix Mobile and Helix Online stayed at "pending" for up to 48 minutes and notifications were delayed. About 1,900 status updates failed on the first attempt and were retried successfully.

This is the second PSS consumer lag incident, after INC-2026-017. The fixes from that incident made a clear difference. Consumer autoscaling, live since late July 2026, added consumers straight away. Notifications were on a separate consumer. Idempotency keys in PayHub and Helix Mobile stopped 412 repeat attempts from becoming duplicate payments. The incident was kept at SEV3.

Lag still built up because autoscaling reached 24 consumers, the same as the number of partitions on `payments.status`. In Kafka, extra consumers beyond the partition count sit idle, so PSS could not scale any further.

## Root cause

The volume of status events on a Friday afternoon, made higher by a bulk payment batch finishing at the same time, needed more processing than 24 consumers could give. The topic was created with 24 partitions in 2025, when volumes were about 40% lower. Partition count is a hard ceiling on how many consumers can share the work. Autoscaling had reached that ceiling, so adding instances would not have helped.

About 1,900 status updates failed because database writes from PSS hit short timeouts while the backlog was cleared. PSS retried them correctly.

**Cause category:** Kafka consumer lag in the Payment Status Service (second occurrence, after INC-2026-017).

## Resolution

The on-call engineer turned on status-only processing, which cut the work per message, and the lag cleared once the bulk peak passed. `payments.status` still has 24 partitions. Increasing the count needs a planned migration because it changes how payment events are spread across consumers, and this is being designed.

## Lessons learned

- The INC-2026-017 fixes worked. Idempotency keys prevented what would have been hundreds of duplicate payments from Helix Mobile.
- The only duplicates came from Helix Online, which shows why finishing idempotency keys there matters.
- Autoscaling is limited by the partition count. Partitions must be planned ahead of volume growth, not after.
